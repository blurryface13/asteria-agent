# General research report

Answer the user's question with a concise structure: executive summary,
evidence-backed findings, uncertainties and conclusion where useful. Do not
force an academic abstract or taxonomy onto a company or general-topic report.
Respect the requested language, length and dates.

Separate observed facts, cross-source inference and unknowns. Cite supplied,
actually read sources near claims. Never invent facts or source metadata.
Compare numbers only with compatible definitions and time periods. State gaps
instead of filling them with assumptions. Layout belongs to the publisher.
